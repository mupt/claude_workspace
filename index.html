About F.M.

F.M. Bonnevier is a Moorestown community member with a deep commitment to public education and the future of Moorestown Township's students. A proud product of public schools, F.M. knows firsthand how transformative a great education can be.

F.M. believes our school board must be responsive, transparent, and focused on student outcomes — not politics. That means listening to parents, supporting teachers, and making data-driven decisions with taxpayer money.

If elected, F.M. will be an independent voice on the board, free from special-interest influence and always accountable to the Moorestown community.

Platform

📚

Academic Excellence

Raise the bar for student achievement in reading, math, and critical thinking. Ensure every Moorestown student is prepared for college and career.

💰

Fiscal Responsibility

Spend taxpayer dollars wisely and transparently. Every dollar should reach the classroom, not administrative overhead or wasteful programs.

🏫

Safe Schools

Ensure every student feels safe and supported. Strengthen mental health resources, anti-bullying programs, and campus safety measures.

🤝

Community Partnership

Keep parents informed and involved. Hold open board meetings, publish clear budgets, and build genuine two-way communication with families.

👩‍🏫

Supporting Teachers

Recruit and retain the best educators by valuing their work, reducing administrative burden, and providing the resources they need to succeed.

🌐

Modern Learning

Thoughtfully integrate technology and STEM opportunities while protecting student data privacy and ensuring equitable access for all students.
